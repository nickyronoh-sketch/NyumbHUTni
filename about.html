About NyumbHUTni

Transforming Access to Safe Water, Sanitation and Hygiene Solutions

Practical Solutions. Better Living. Healthier Communities.

Who We Are

NyumbHUTni is a solutions-driven social enterprise dedicated to improving access to safe drinking water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) solutions for households, businesses, institutions and communities.

We believe that access to safe water, proper sanitation and good hygiene is fundamental to healthier communities, productive businesses and improved quality of life. Guided by this belief, we bring together reliable products, practical technologies and accessible solutions that address everyday water, sanitation and hygiene challenges.

Our approach is simple: make essential solutions accessible, affordable and convenient by connecting our customers to the right products, technologies and services.

Through our online platform and physical retail outlet in Bomet, we serve individuals, businesses, institutions and organizations seeking dependable WASH solutions.

What We Do

We provide a comprehensive range of water, sanitation and hygiene products and solutions, including:

1

Water Purification and Treatment Solutions

We supply water purification technologies designed to improve access to clean and safe drinking water. Our solutions include water filtration and purification systems for homes, businesses, institutions and other applications.

2

Water Pumping and Supply Solutions

We provide water pumping technologies and related accessories to support efficient water extraction, distribution and supply for domestic, commercial and institutional use.

3

Sanitation Solutions

We offer sanitation products and accessories that support improved sanitation infrastructure, functionality and convenience. Our range includes toilets, toilet accessories and bathroom fittings.

4

Hygiene Products and Solutions

We provide practical hygiene products and accessories that promote cleaner, safer and healthier living and working environments.

5

Solar Solutions

We offer solar-powered solutions that support energy access and the efficient operation of water-related systems and other applications.

6

Water Quality Testing

We facilitate access to water quality testing solutions and technologies, helping customers assess the quality of their water and make informed decisions about appropriate treatment and purification options.

Promotional Branding and Marketing Services

Beyond our WASH solutions, NyumbHUTni also provides promotional branding services to businesses, institutions, organizations and other entities seeking to strengthen their visibility and brand identity.

We support organizations in creating brand awareness through promotional products, branded merchandise and customized branding solutions.

We understand that a strong brand is built through consistent visibility and meaningful engagement. Our goal is to help businesses and organizations communicate their identity, promote their products and services, and connect more effectively with their audiences.

This business arm complements our broader commitment to providing practical solutions that support organizational growth and visibility.

Our Commitment

At NyumbHUTni, we are driven by the belief that access to essential services should not be unnecessarily complicated. We are committed to:

  • Accessibility: Making essential water, sanitation and hygiene products more accessible to households, businesses and institutions.
  • Quality and Reliability: Connecting customers with dependable products and practical technologies.
  • Convenience: Simplifying product discovery, purchasing and access to solutions through our online and physical retail channels.
  • Community Impact: Contributing to improved access to safe drinking water, better sanitation and healthier living environments.
  • Customer-Centred Service: Understanding our customers' needs and helping them identify appropriate solutions.

Our Vision

A society where every household, business and institution has improved access to safe drinking water, dignified sanitation and sustainable hygiene solutions.

Our Mission

To make reliable water, sanitation and hygiene solutions accessible through quality products, practical technologies, innovation and customer-centred service, while supporting businesses and organizations through effective promotional branding solutions.
